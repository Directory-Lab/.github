<h1 align="center">DirectoryLab</h1>

<p align="center"><strong>Niche site kits that earn for years.</strong></p>

Three production codebases for niche sites: local directories, job boards and launch platforms. They are the same engines behind my own portfolio of sites. Each kit is a complete business in a box: the public site, the admin, the payments and the automation. Pick a niche, set it up in the admin, and it is yours to run.

| **Launch Lab** | Launch platforms, in the style of Product Hunt | [Kit page](https://directorylab.io/launch-lab) · [Docs](https://directorylab.io/docs/launch-lab) |
